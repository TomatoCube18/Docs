## Box08 - X'mas Snow Globe TableTop Toy

<img src="Images/SnowGlobe_Box.PNG" alt="IMG_5882_Posting" width="500" height="auto"/>

A programmable electronic snow globe with a festive winter scene, perfect for learning embedded systems and making your holiday decorations interactive. ❄️🎅✨ The SnowGlobe TableTop Toy comes in three different variants, each carefully thought out to align with various coding/maker skill level.
<br><br>

### Quick Start

Assemble the Snow Globe using the PDF user manual or the optional visual walkthrough below.
- Snow Globe Assembly Guide – **[Step-by-step Video](https://youtu.be/-vwsVRtAr5w)**

Select your edition below and follow the dedicated user guide to set up, program, and customise your snow globe:
- Micro:bit v2 Edition – Ideal for beginners **[User Manual](microbit%20v2%20Edition/Files/SnowGlobe_microBit_UserManual.pdf)** 
- Raspberry Pi Pico Edition – Great for intermediate makers **[User Manual/Quick Start Guide](Raspberry%20Pi%20Pico%20Edition/Files/SnowGlobe_Pico_UserManual.pdf)** & **[Pico SnowGlobe CircuitPython Mini Camp](Raspberry%20Pi%20Pico%20Edition/Files/SnowGlobe_Pico_CircuitPython_Mini-Camp.md)**
- WCH CH32x033 Edition – For advanced tinkerers looking to stretch their engineering muscles

<br>
