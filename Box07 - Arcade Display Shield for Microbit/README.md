## Box07 - Arcade Display Shield for micro:bit

Welcome to your **Arcade Display Shield**, a vibrant add-on designed to bring the pixel-perfect graphics of MakeCode Arcade to the popular BBC micro:bit. Transform your micro:bit into a dedicated handheld gaming console with a bright, colorful 160x128 display and buttons.
